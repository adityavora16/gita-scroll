What the app is

Gita Scroll is a reading app. It shows verses of the Bhagavad Gita chosen by the topics you pick, with translations, meanings, stories, quizzes, chanting and recitation audio, and an optional feature called Gita Lock.

Where the text comes from

The Sanskrit verses come from public-domain sources. The translations, meanings, stories and explanations are original to Gita Scroll. The classical commentators' views are summarised from public-domain translations and shown side by side, not as the single correct reading. Each verse is marked "Draft, under review" until a human Sanskrit reviewer approves it, so some text may contain mistakes. You can report a problem from the verse screen. The developer will fix what it can, and corrections reach you in an app update.

Gita Scroll does not belong to or speak for any one tradition, teacher or organisation.
